About

Lydia Plante is a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at Clemson University (GPA 3.44). She serves on the Structures, Simulations, and Recovery teams in Clemson University Rocket Engineering, designing internal and external structures, and manufacturing composite components.

As a certified supervisor at the Cook Mechanical Engineering Lab, she trains students on lathes, mills, welders, and other fabrication equipment daily — reinforcing a detail-oriented, safety-first engineering mindset.

Her interests span aerospace systems, energy systems, robotics, and theme attraction engineering.

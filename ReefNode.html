ReefNode Aquarium Controller

SUMMARY

ReefNode is a personal aquarium monitoring and automation project inspired by my goal of building and maintaining a 200+ gallon saltwater aquarium in my future home.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

A large saltwater aquarium requires consistent monitoring and maintenance to support a healthy environment. I wanted to explore how a custom system could bring essential readings and equipment status together in one accessible interface.

APPROACH

I’m developing a Raspberry Pi–based controller in Python alongside an HTML, CSS, and JavaScript dashboard. The current prototype simulates water temperature, water level, air temperature, humidity, and equipment responses as a foundation for future sensors and automation.

RESULTS

The working simulation displays readings, alerts, and heater, fan, and automatic top-off states in a dashboard. A public demo shows the interface with example data, while sensor integration and additional safeguards are planned as the project develops.
